Mostly Messages Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026 Applies to the Mostly Messages app for Android

Mostly Messages turns a phone or tablet into a shared message board for a household or workplace. This policy explains what information the app handles, where that information goes, the third-party services involved, and the choices you have. In short: your email, calendar, notes and contacts are processed on your device and are not sent to us.

At a glance

No app servers

We don't run servers that receive your email, calendar, notes or contacts. The app talks directly to the services you connect.

Stored on your device

What the app keeps is stored on the device, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore and excluded from device backups.

Never sold

We don't sell your information, and email and calendar content is never used for advertising.

Ads by Google

The board shows a banner ad from Google AdMob, which collects device and usage data under Google's own policies.

Information the app handles

The app only handles the information needed for the features you use. Each feature is described below.

Email account

You connect one email account: either a Google account with Gmail, or another account that offers IMAP and SMTP.

  • Account identity. The email address of the connected account, to show which account is in use.
  • Unread Inbox messages. Sender, subject, date and message body of unread messages in the Inbox that match the board's filters, so they can be shown on the board.
  • Read status. When someone presses Mark read, the app marks that one message as read in the account.
  • Messages you send. The app sends email only when someone presses Send in the reply or share-contacts form, and only to the addresses entered there. Sent messages appear in the account's Sent folder.
  • IMAP/SMTP password. For other email accounts, the password (usually an app password) is stored on the device only and sent only to the mail servers you entered, over an encrypted connection. This includes Gmail and Google Workspace accounts connected with a Google app password: the app password goes only to Google’s mail servers, and no Google API or Google sign-in is used for them.

The app does not delete, archive or move email, and does not change labels or folders other than marking a message read.

Calendar

Calendar features are optional. With a Google account, if you turn calendar on, the app reads your calendar list and events (title, time, reminders, location, description, organizer, guests and meeting link) to show events on the board when their reminder is due and in the day view. It creates, changes or deletes an event only when someone saves an event, edits one, or confirms a deletion. It never replies to invitations. Events for other days are loaded only while you view that day and are not stored. With an IMAP/SMTP account, events can be kept on the device only (“This board”, never synced). If you also allow Android’s Calendar permission, the app reads the calendars stored on the device (for example Google or Outlook calendars added in Android Settings) in the same way, and adds, changes or deletes events in them only when someone does so on the board. The app reads and writes them on the device; any syncing is done by Android and the accounts you added, not by us, and nothing is sent to us. You can withdraw the permission in Android Settings at any time.

Notes and emergency contacts

Notes typed on the board, and emergency contacts you enter (names, relationships, phone numbers and email addresses), are stored only on the device. Emergency contacts leave the device only if someone chooses to share them by email, to the recipients they enter.

Weather

If weather is on (it is on by default), the clock shows the current weather and today's forecast for a U.S. city or ZIP code you choose. The app sends that place's map coordinates, the center of the city or ZIP code you picked, to the U.S. National Weather Service. The app does not use your device's location and does not ask for location permission.

Settings

Board settings such as colors, timings, the sender whitelist, the subject keyword and the weather location are stored on the device and excluded from cloud backup.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide the app's features on your device:

  • to show selected messages, notes, calendar events and weather on the board;
  • to mark messages read, send the replies and shares people write, and save calendar changes people make;
  • to keep the board working when the connection drops briefly, using an encrypted cache.

We do not use your email, calendar, notes or contacts for advertising, analytics or profiling, and no person at our end reads them. The app does not include crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not log message content, passwords or tokens. The only measurement it includes is Meta's app events, which tell us when the app was installed or opened after a Facebook or Instagram ad; see Advertising.

Advertising (Google AdMob and Meta)

Mostly Messages is free and shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and how ads are viewed and tapped. Google may use this information to personalize ads, where you allow it.

What ads never receive Ad requests never include your email, calendar events, notes, emergency contacts or the weather location you chose.

Where required by law, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to show a consent message before ads are personalized. Google processes this information under its own policies:

Measuring our Facebook and Instagram ads (Meta)

We advertise the app on Facebook and Instagram. Google user data

When you connect a Google account, the app asks for permission to read and modify Gmail (needed to read messages, mark them read and send the messages people write) and, separately and only if you turn calendar on, to read your calendars and to create and change events. You can see and remove this access at any time in your Google Account permissions.

Limited Use Mostly Messages's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is not transferred to third parties, is not used for advertising, and is not read by people, except where you ask us for help and give permission, where needed for security, or where the law requires it.

Storage, security and retention

  • Account details, cached messages, calendar data, notes, emergency contacts, IMAP/SMTP passwords and the last weather are stored on the device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key held in the Android Keystore, and excluded from device backups and device-to-device transfer.
  • All network connections use encryption (HTTPS or TLS), with certificate checks.
  • Cached messages are removed when they no longer belong on the board, for example once read or archived.
  • Disconnect deletes the account's sign-in details and all cached email and calendar data from the device.
  • Notes and emergency contacts stay until you delete them in the app; they are not removed by Disconnect.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the device.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and a device that is unlocked and left in a shared space can be used by anyone nearby. The board is designed to be seen by the people around it, so choose your filters with that in mind.

Your choices and controls

  • Choose what appears. Limit the board to approved senders or a subject keyword in Settings.
  • Disconnect. Remove the connected account and its cached data in Settings.
  • Revoke Google access. Remove the app's access in your Google Account.
  • Calendar. Turn calendar off in Settings at any time. With an IMAP/SMTP account, remove the Calendar permission in Android Settings > Apps > Mostly Messages > Permissions to stop the app reading the calendars on the device.
  • Weather. Turn weather off in Settings > Weather to stop all weather requests.
  • Ads. Manage ad personalization and your advertising ID as described in Advertising.
  • Delete everything. Uninstall the app.

Limits of this policy

This policy describes how the Mostly Messages app itself handles information. Some parts of the experience are provided by others, and we want to be clear about where our knowledge and control stop:

  • Third-party services. Google, your email provider, the National Weather Service, Google AdMob and Meta operate their own services. We describe what the app sends them, based on how the app is built and on their published documentation, but we do not control and cannot fully verify how they collect, use, retain or share information. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Advertising SDKs. The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Meta's Facebook SDK are provided by Google and Meta and run inside the app. The information they collect is determined by Google and Meta and may change with SDK updates. Their documentation linked above is the authoritative description.
  • Android and Google Play. Your device's operating system, Google Play services and Google Play may collect information, such as crash reports and diagnostics if you allow them, under their own terms. If you share diagnostics, Google may make aggregated crash reports available to us; these describe app errors and are not intended to contain your messages.
  • People at the board. Anyone who can see or use the device can see what the board shows, open messages and mark them read. The app cannot control who has physical access to the device.
  • Email you send. Once a reply or shared contact list is sent, it is handled by your email provider and the recipients' providers, outside the app's control.

Children

Mostly Messages is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Connecting an account is intended for the adult who owns it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Because the app stores your information on your device rather than on our servers, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app (Disconnect, delete notes and contacts) or by uninstalling it. For information held by Google, your email provider or the National Weather Service, please use their privacy tools. For anything else, contact us using the details below.

We do not sell personal information.
