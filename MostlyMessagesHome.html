A shared message board for your home or business

Mostly Messages is an Android app that turns a phone or tablet into a shared message board. It shows the emails you choose, notes, today's calendar events, the time and the weather in large, easy-to-read text, on a screen everyone can see: in the kitchen, at the front desk, in the break room or on the shop floor.

What it does

Set it up once, put the device on a counter, desk or wall, and it keeps everyone up to date, whether that's a household, an office or a small team.

Only the emails you choose

Show emails from approved senders, or with a subject keyword such as "Board:". Everything else in your inbox stays private.

Stays until it's dealt with

Messages rotate on the board until someone opens one and presses Mark read. Anyone can reply to the sender right from the board.

Calendar and today's schedule

Events join the board when their reminder is due, and a day view shows what's on today. Add or change events from the board.

Notes

Leave a note right on the board, like "Bins go out tonight" or "Staff meeting moved to 3 PM". It shows up with the messages.

Clock and weather

A big clock with the current weather and today's forecast from the U.S. National Weather Service, plus a count of what's waiting.

Emergency contacts

Keep important names and numbers one tap away for babysitters, visitors, staff and contractors, and share the list by email.

How it works

There's no account to create with us, and no app servers. The app talks directly to the services you connect.

  1. Install

    Install Mostly Messages from Google Play on a phone or tablet you can leave out.

  2. Connect email

    Sign in with Google's own account picker for Gmail, or enter your provider's IMAP/SMTP settings.

  3. Choose what shows

    Pick approved senders or a subject keyword, then set colors, timings and the weather location.

  4. Start the board

    Press Display Messageboard. Family, coworkers or customers send email to the account, and it appears on the board.

How Mostly Messages uses your Google account

When you choose Gmail, the app asks for these permissions, using Google's sign-in. Each one is used only to provide the board features described here.

PermissionWhy the app needs it
Gmail: read, compose, send and modify email
gmail.modify		 To find the emails that match your board settings and show them, to mark an email read when someone presses Mark read, and to send the replies and contact lists people write on the board. The app never deletes your email.
Calendar: see your calendar list and events
calendar.calendarlist.readonly, calendar.events.readonly		 Only if you turn calendar on. Used to show today's events on the board when their reminder is due, and in the day view.
Calendar: view and edit events
calendar.events		 Only requested when you first add or edit an event from the board. Used to save that event to the calendar you choose.
  • Your email and calendar data go directly from Google to your device. We don't run servers that receive them.
  • What the app keeps is stored on the device, encrypted, and is deleted when you press Disconnect or uninstall the app.
  • Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never transferred to others.
  • You can remove the app's access at any time in your Google Account permissions.
